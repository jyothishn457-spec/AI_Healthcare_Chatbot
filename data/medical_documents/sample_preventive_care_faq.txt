PREVENTIVE CARE AND HEALTH FAQ

HOW MUCH WATER SHOULD I DRINK DAILY?
Most healthy adults should aim for about 8 glasses (roughly 2 litres) of water a day. Needs vary with activity level, climate and certain health conditions. Dark urine is a common sign that you may need more fluids.

WHAT ARE GOOD HEART-HEALTHY HABITS?
Eat a balanced diet rich in vegetables, fruits, whole grains and healthy fats. Exercise for at least 30 minutes on most days. Maintain a healthy weight, avoid smoking, limit alcohol, manage stress and get regular blood pressure and cholesterol checks.

HOW MANY HOURS OF SLEEP DO I NEED?
Adults generally need 7 to 9 hours of quality sleep per night. Good sleep hygiene includes a fixed schedule, a cool dark room, and avoiding screens for about an hour before bed.

WHAT CAN I DO TO PREVENT INFECTIONS?
Wash your hands frequently with soap and water for at least 20 seconds. Keep recommended vaccinations up to date. Cover coughs and sneezes, avoid touching your face, and stay home when you are sick.

HOW OFTEN SHOULD I EXERCISE?
Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity per week (about 30 minutes on five days), plus strength training twice a week. Check with your doctor before starting a new program if you have existing health conditions.

IS STRESS BAD FOR MY HEALTH?
Chronic stress can raise blood pressure and weaken the immune system. Managing stress through deep breathing, meditation, physical activity and talking with others supports overall health.
